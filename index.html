Cool Snail at a glance

Questions

How do I block Instagram Reels on Android without deleting Instagram?

Install Cool Snail and turn on its Accessibility access. When you open full-screen Reels, Cool Snail takes you back out after about a second. Your feed, stories, DMs, search and posting keep working, so you don't have to delete Instagram.

How do I block YouTube Shorts on Android?

Cool Snail detects the YouTube Shorts player and takes you out of it. Long videos, subscriptions and search work as normal.

Is there a free app to stop scrolling TikTok?

Yes. Cool Snail is free on Android with no trial and no time limit. Open TikTok's video feed and it takes you out after a second of grace. You can also give TikTok its own daily limit.

Can I still watch a video a friend sends me?

Yes. If you open a Reel, Short or TikTok from a chat on WhatsApp, Telegram, Messenger or Signal, it plays and a small note says: Sent by a friend. Go ahead. On Instagram, swiping to a reel from someone else ends the pass; anywhere, leaving the video or switching apps ends it.

Does Cool Snail work in the browser?

Yes, in Chrome: short videos opened in Chrome, such as TikTok links, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels, take you out too.

Why does Cool Snail need Accessibility permission?

Android only lets an app see that you have opened a Reel, a Short or a TikTok through its Accessibility service. Cool Snail uses it only inside the social apps it supports, to count your time and take you out of short videos. It does not read other apps, and your statistics stay on your phone.

Can I get more time when I reach my limit?

On the free version, yes, but it costs you something: when you reach your daily limit or an app's own limit, you can watch one ad to get 5 more minutes, starting from that moment. It is friction on purpose, so going over is a choice and not a reflex. Open hours can't be extended, and PRO removes the ad option entirely.

Can I choose the hours when social apps are allowed?

Yes. Set open hours with a start and an end time, for example 08:00 to 23:00. They are the same every day. Outside that window your social apps close. A window that goes past midnight carries on into the next morning.

Is Cool Snail really free?

Yes. Blocking short videos, daily and per-app limits, open hours, the bubble and today's statistics are free forever, with no account and no card. When you go over your limit, you can watch one ad for 5 more minutes.

What does Cool Snail PRO add?

PRO fixes your limits, open hours and switches for 30 days, so you can't loosen them until the 30 days are over. It removes the ad extension and unlocks your full statistics: the last 7 and 30 days, this week against the last one, and the hour you lose most. It is a monthly or yearly subscription at local Google Play prices; in Spain, 3.49 euros a month or 34.99 euros a year.

How is Cool Snail different from Digital Wellbeing app timers?

An app timer pauses the whole app, so you lose your messages too. Cool Snail only goes after the short-video feed and leaves messages, search and stories alone. It can also limit whole apps when you want that.

Is there an iPhone version of Cool Snail?

Not yet. Cool Snail is Android only, for Android 8.0 and later.

Cool Snail stopped working on my Xiaomi or Samsung phone. What do I do?

Some phones switch accessibility services off to save battery. Set Cool Snail's battery use to Unrestricted (No restrictions on Xiaomi), allow Autostart if your phone has it, then turn Cool Snail back on in Accessibility settings.
