Surgical, not punishing

Most blockers lock the whole app, so you turn them off the first time you need to answer a message. Cool Snail only goes after the feed built to keep you there.

Friction, not a wall

A blocker you can't live with gets uninstalled. These three make it something you keep.

Friends get through

A reel from a friend plays

Open a Reel, Short or TikTok from a chat on WhatsApp, Telegram, Messenger, Signal or Instagram's own DMs and it plays. The snail peeks in with a small note, Sent by a friend. Go ahead., and leaves you to it.

Only videos opened straight from a chat get the pass. On Instagram, swiping to a reel from someone else ends it; anywhere, leaving the video or switching apps ends it.

Open hours

Your social apps keep office hours

Pick a start and an end, say 08:00 to 23:00. Outside that window Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and the rest close, and a card tells you when they open again.

The same hours every day. A window past midnight carries into the next morning.

The price of five minutes

Over your limit? Watch an ad

Give each app its own minutes, plus one daily total. When you run out, the app closes. If you really want more, one ad buys 5 minutes, counted from that moment.

Friction on purpose: going over becomes a choice, not a reflex. Every ad shows up in your statistics. Open hours can't be bought, and PRO removes the ad option.

Questions

What does Cool Snail do?

Cool Snail gives you four tools against social media addiction, each with its own switch. Exit shorts pulls you out of Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok and the X video tab. Time blocking sets a daily limit, a limit per app and open hours. One page at a time breaks up infinite scrolling on Reddit and X with a 3-second pause every 20 posts. The bubble floats over the feed with your time today and the posts you've seen.

How do I block Instagram Reels on Android without deleting Instagram?

Install Cool Snail and turn on its Accessibility access. When you open full-screen Reels, Cool Snail takes you back out after about a second. Your feed, stories, DMs, search and posting keep working, so you don't have to delete Instagram.

How do I block YouTube Shorts on Android?

Cool Snail detects the YouTube Shorts player and takes you out of it. Long videos, subscriptions and search work as normal.

Is there a free app to stop scrolling TikTok?

Yes. Cool Snail is free on Android with no trial and no time limit. Open TikTok's video feed and it takes you out after a second of grace. You can also give TikTok its own daily limit.

Can I still watch a video a friend sends me?

Yes. If you open a Reel, Short or TikTok from a chat on WhatsApp, Telegram, Messenger, Signal or Instagram's own DMs, it plays and a small note says: Sent by a friend. Go ahead. On Instagram, swiping to a reel from someone else ends the pass; anywhere, leaving the video or switching apps ends it. Only videos opened straight from a chat get the pass.

Does Cool Snail work in the browser?

Yes, in Chrome: short videos opened in Chrome, such as TikTok links, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels, take you out too.

Why does Cool Snail need Accessibility permission?

Android only lets an app see that you have opened a Reel, a Short or a TikTok through its Accessibility service. Cool Snail uses it only inside the social apps it supports, to count your time and take you out of short videos. What it sees there is processed on your phone and never sent anywhere.

Can I get more time when I reach my limit?

On the free version, yes, but it costs you something: when you reach your daily limit or an app's own limit, you can watch one ad to get 5 more minutes, starting from that moment. It is friction on purpose, so going over is a choice and not a reflex. Open hours can't be extended, and PRO removes the ad option entirely.

Can I choose the hours when social apps are allowed?

Yes. Set open hours with a start and an end time, for example 08:00 to 23:00. They are the same every day. Outside that window your social apps close. A window that goes past midnight carries on into the next morning.

Is Cool Snail really free?

Yes. Blocking short videos, daily and per-app limits, open hours, the bubble and today's statistics are free forever, with no account and no card. When you go over your limit, you can watch one ad for 5 more minutes.

What does Cool Snail PRO add?

PRO is for fixing your rules: your limits, open hours and switches are fixed for 30 days and can't be loosened until the 30 days are over. You confirm it in the app before it starts, so it never begins by surprise. PRO also removes the ad extension and unlocks your full statistics: the last 7 and 30 days, this week against the last one, and the hour you lose most. It is a monthly or yearly subscription at local Google Play prices; in Spain, 3.49 euros a month or 34.99 euros a year.

How is Cool Snail different from Digital Wellbeing app timers?

An app timer pauses the whole app, so you lose your messages too. Cool Snail only goes after the short-video feed and leaves messages, search and stories alone. It can also limit whole apps when you want that.

Does Cool Snail collect my data?

Some, and the privacy policy lists all of it. What you do inside your social apps, your time, the posts you see and your statistics stay on your phone and are deleted when you uninstall the app. What does leave the phone: your purchase history and an anonymous ID, through Google Play and RevenueCat. On the free plan, only if you choose to watch the optional ad, Google AdMob receives your advertising ID, your IP address and how you interact with the ad, with your consent where the law requires it. OneSignal receives a device token to deliver notifications, and your email only if you join the gifting waitlist.

What happens if Instagram, YouTube or TikTok change their app?

Cool Snail recognises Reels, Shorts and TikTok by how those apps are built, so a big redesign can stop it from spotting a feed until Cool Snail is updated. When that happens it gets fixed in an app update; TikTok's September 2026 redesign was fixed within days.

Is there an iPhone version of Cool Snail?

Not yet. Cool Snail is Android only, for Android 8.0 and later.

Cool Snail stopped working on my Xiaomi or Samsung phone. What do I do?

Some phones switch accessibility services off to save battery. Set Cool Snail's battery use to Unrestricted (No restrictions on Xiaomi), allow Autostart if your phone has it, then turn Cool Snail back on in Accessibility settings.
