Best apps to block social media addiction on Android (2026)

By Miriam, who makes Cool Snail · Updated 12 October 2026

Short answer

It depends on what you want gone. To pause whole apps on a timer, Android's own Digital Wellbeing is free and already on your phone. To block whole apps by schedule, location or Wi-Fi, a blocker like AppBlock does that. To keep Instagram, YouTube and TikTok but lose the endless video feeds, Cool Snail (free) takes you out of Reels, Shorts and TikTok and cuts Reddit and X into pages, while messages and search keep working.

Three ways to fight the scroll

Most tools fall into one of three groups. Some limit how long a whole app can be open. Some block whole apps and websites under rules you set. A few go after the parts of an app built to keep you there, like the Reels tab or an endless feed, and leave the rest alone. Which one helps depends on whether the app is the problem or only one part of it.

Why Cool Snail

  • Four tools, each with its own switch. Block short videos, control your time (one daily limit for all social apps, a limit per app, open hours), break up infinite scrolling on Reddit and X, and a bubble over the feed with today's time and posts seen.

  • Free, no account. No trial, no sign-up, no card. PRO is optional: it fixes your rules for 30 days so you can't loosen them, and unlocks full statistics.
  • Your activity stays on your phone. Time, posts seen and statistics are processed and stored only on the device and deleted when you uninstall. The details are in the privacy policy.

When another option is better

  • You want a whole app gone during certain hours or places, like social media off at work or at the gym: a whole-app blocker with schedules and location rules, such as AppBlock, is made for that.
  • You only need a gentle timer and don't want to install anything: Digital Wellbeing's app timers and Focus mode are already on most Android phones.
  • You use an iPhone: Cool Snail is Android only. iPhone has Screen Time built in, and AppBlock also has an iPhone version.
  • You want to block websites in general: Cool Snail only acts on short videos opened in Chrome (TikTok links, Reels, Shorts, Facebook Reels), not on other sites.

Limits you should know

Cool Snail recognises Reels, Shorts and TikTok by how those apps are built, so a redesign can break detection until Cool Snail is updated. On Xiaomi, Redmi, POCO and Samsung phones, set its battery use to Unrestricted so Android doesn't stop it.

Questions

Is Digital Wellbeing enough to stop doomscrolling?

For some people, yes. Its app timers pause a whole app until midnight and Focus mode pauses chosen apps. It can't tell Reels from your DMs, so a pause stops both.

Can I block Reels and Shorts but keep Instagram and YouTube?

Yes, with Cool Snail on Android. It takes you out of Reels, Shorts and TikTok's video feed about a second after they open; DMs, stories, long videos and search keep working.

Do I need to pay?

Not for Cool Snail: it's free forever with no account. PRO is an optional subscription that fixes your rules for 30 days and unlocks full statistics. Digital Wellbeing is free; AppBlock has a free version and a Premium plan.
